🎁 Daily Bonus & Earning System

Your earnings are calculated based on the number of students who register and get approved on the same day. The cycle resets every day.

  • 1st Student: ₹20
  • 2nd Student: ₹20
  • 3rd Student: ₹20 + ₹40 Bonus
  • 4th to 6th Student: ₹30 each
  • 7th Student: ₹30 + ₹120 Bonus
  • 8th onwards: ₹30 each

Note: For example, if you get 2 approved students today, you earn ₹40. To get the ₹40 bonus (Total ₹100), you need 3 approved students in a single day.
